# Hills Coffee / Hills Coffee Trading — Design Planning Brief

## Objective
Create an exceptionally polished, premium, modern, highly structured website design plan for **Hills Coffee / Hills Coffee Trading**.

The goal is to produce a **design strategy and implementation plan first**, not immediate coding.

This work must be grounded in **Refero research** before any design decisions are made.

---

## Important Working Rule
Use **Tomorro** as a **style reference only**, not as a clone target.

We want to borrow inspiration from its:
- dark/light alternating section rhythm
- premium editorial feel
- pill-based navigation and CTA styling
- restrained but vivid green accent usage
- elegant typography-led hierarchy
- high spacing quality
- polished presentation of floating surfaces and subtle atmosphere

But the final result must be clearly adapted to **Hills Coffee**, its industry, its audience, and its content.

Do **not** reproduce Tomorro literally.
Do **not** copy layouts section-by-section.
Do **not** copy brand language or product framing from SaaS.
Instead, reinterpret the visual language for a **premium green coffee sourcing and trading company**.

---

## Brand / Business Context
Brand: **Hills Coffee / Hills Coffee Trading**

Business positioning:
- Premium B2B green coffee sourcing / trading company
- Serves the Arab region / MENA market
- Focused on quality, sourcing, traceability, warehousing, logistics, and long-term business trust
- Professional, high-end, credible, clean, modern
- Not a casual café brand
- Not a lifestyle coffee shop
- Not a retail DTC coffee brand
- More “premium sourcing / trade / logistics / quality partner” than “coffeehouse”

Primary audience:
- importers
- roasters
- distributors
- wholesalers
- business buyers
- hospitality / F&B operators
- partners looking for reliable sourcing and supply

Primary business goals:
- Build trust
- Communicate premium quality and credibility
- Present Hills Coffee as organized, refined, and modern
- Make services easy to understand
- Improve conversion toward inquiry / contact / quote / partnership actions

   - cards / badges / data or feature layouts

3. **Flows**  
   If relevant, inspect journey logic for:
   - lead generation

## Color Direction
Base the design system on this palette direction.

### Core colors
- Forest Depths: `#122314`
- Moss Shadow: `#273f2b`
- Lichen Sage: `#7e8371`
- Pale Fern: `#b7bda5`
- Hills Burnt Orange: `#a44819` (Approved primary accent replacing `#68ef3f`)
- Deep Burnt Orange: `#8c3a12`
- Orange Wash: `#fdf5f0`
- Mist Green: `#d9deca`
- Onyx Olive: `#30322a`
- Pure White: `#ffffff`
- Bone White: `#f2f5eb`
- Soft Mist: `#dcdfe3`
- Cool Stone: `#d6d6d6`
- Carbon: `#222222`

### Color usage rules
- Use **Electric Sprout** as the main action accent color
- Avoid adding extra accent hues unless absolutely necessary
- Keep the palette disciplined
- Alternate between dark and light sections
- Use Bone White for elevated light surfaces
- Keep green usage intentional, not overwhelming
- Avoid gradients as primary page backgrounds; use them only sparingly for atmosphere if needed

---

## Typography Direction
If licensed fonts are unavailable, use strong **free substitutes**.

### Preferred mood
- Display: bold, tight, editorial, high-impact
- UI/body: clean, modern, highly readable
- Accent serif: occasional editorial warmth only

### Preferred stack
If available:
- Display: **Ozik**
- UI/body: **Aeonik**
- Accent serif: **Instrument Serif**

If unavailable, use free substitutes:
- Display fallback: **Syne** or **Bricolage Grotesque**
- UI/body fallback: **Geist** or **Inter**
- Accent serif fallback: **Instrument Serif** or **Newsreader**

### Typography behavior
- Hero display should feel premium and compressed
- Body text should remain calm and readable
- Accent serif should be rare and intentional
- Do not overuse bold body text
- Use size, spacing, and color emphasis more than heavy font-weight everywhere

---

## Visual Composition Direction
We want the final site to be:
- mostly typography-led
- supported by refined surfaces
- supported by premium coffee-related imagery where helpful
- supported by selective atmospheric visuals

Recommended composition mix:
- 60–70% typography and layout discipline
- 15–20% premium imagery / coffee sourcing visuals
- 10–15% cards / trust blocks / structured content surfaces
- 5–10% subtle atmospheric elements (orb, soft texture, motion, depth)

Important:
- Do not make it feel like SaaS software screenshots are the product
- Adapt visual storytelling to **green coffee sourcing / trading**
- Use:
  - green coffee beans
  - quality inspection
  - sourcing / traceability themes
  - warehousing / logistics cues
  - trade credibility
  - maps / origins / cards / process storytelling
- Avoid:
  - cheesy café imagery
  - generic stock photos
  - overly busy collage sections
  - random decorative elements
  - AI-slop visuals
